Her Story

From science to soul.

Dr. Ritika began her career convinced that medicine had to be either gentle or effective — never both. Years of practice changed her mind. The most lasting healing she witnessed came from understanding the person, not just the diagnosis.

She trained classically in Homoeopathy and then specialised in Clinical Nutrition & Dietetics — a dual credential that lets her treat the body's chemistry and its constitution at the same time. Today she runs Navjeevan Homoeopathic Clinic in Mumbai, with patients consulting online from across India and abroad.

Her belief is simple: "Small changes today, stronger health tomorrow."
